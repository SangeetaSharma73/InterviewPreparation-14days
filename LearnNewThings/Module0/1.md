# 🔹 1. What is AI?

## 💡 Definition:

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science focused on building systems that can mimic human intelligence to perform tasks such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding.

## 🧠 Key Characteristics of AI:

| Trait           | Explanation                                             |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Learning**    | Improve performance from experience (like humans do).   |
| **Reasoning**   | Make logical decisions based on knowledge/data.         |
| **Perception**  | Understand inputs like images, sounds, or text.         |
| **Interaction** | Communicate or respond like humans (via voice or text). |
| **Autonomy**    | Perform tasks without human intervention.               |
